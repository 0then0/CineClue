# Code of Conduct

CineClue welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Discuss code and ideas respectfully, give constructive feedback and accept good-faith corrections.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, disclosure of private information and unwanted sexual content are not acceptable. These expectations apply in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Contact a maintainer privately through a contact method listed on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/0then0). Include relevant links and context, and avoid publishing another person's private information. If a report concerns a maintainer, contact another uninvolved maintainer when available.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation or ban participants for serious or repeated violations. Responses should be proportionate and explained privately when practical. Maintainers are held to the same standards.
